  • Concept & product design

    Your idea, turned into a product plan.

    We take your idea and turn it into a clear concept, a project plan and a design that can be built. Function, cost and production are part of the design from day one.

    For: founders with an idea; companies planning a new product

  • Industrial design

    Shaped around the people who use it.

    Form, ergonomics, materials and finish, worked out for daily use. We design for the hands, backs and routines of real users, then for the shelf.

    For: products that people touch, carry or work with every day

  • 3D modelling & rendering

    See your product before it exists.

    We build detailed 3D models and photorealistic renderings and animations. Use them to settle details, pitch to investors or start marketing before the first part is made.

    For: founders raising money; marketing and sales teams

Developplan → prototype

  • FEA / FEM simulation

    Know it holds before it is built.

    Finite element analysis shows how parts bend and where stress builds up under load. We use it to size material correctly, so parts are safe without being heavier or more expensive than they need to be.

    For: load-bearing parts, frames, mounts and moving mechanisms

  • 3D printing, prototyping & testing

    Hold it, test it, improve it.

    We 3D print parts and build working prototypes so you can test fit, function and feel early. We test them ourselves, or coordinate testing to the relevant standards with accredited test facilities. Each round brings the product closer to production.

    For: anyone who needs to prove a product before investing in production

  • Engineering & technical documentation

    Engineered to high Austrian standards.

    We engineer every part for strength, safety and manufacturing. Then we document it: drawings, CAD data, calculations and manuals, ready for suppliers, certification and your customers.

    For: companies without in-house engineering capacity

    We set up production using our own metalwork and a network of suppliers, and run the chain for you. You deal with one contact instead of many.

  • Packaging development

    Ready for the shelf.

    We develop packaging that protects the product, suits shipping and presents your brand.

How a project runs

Eight steps, a result after each one.

You see a plan and a budget before anything is built. You can stop after any step and keep what has been made.

  1. 01

    Idea and briefing

    You tell us what the product should do, for whom, and what budget you have in mind.

  2. 02

    Plan and concept

    We return a concept, a project plan and a budget. You decide whether to go ahead.

  3. 03

    Design and 3D models

    Form and function take shape as 3D models and renderings you can review.

  4. 04

    Prototypes and testing

    Printed parts and working prototypes, improved in rounds. We test in-house, or coordinate tests to the relevant standards with accredited test facilities.

  5. 05

    Engineering, FEA and documentation

    Parts are engineered, simulated and documented for production.

  6. 06

    Production and supply chain

    We build in our workshop or with suppliers, and manage the chain.

  7. 07

    Packaging

    Packaging designed to protect, ship and sell.

  8. 08

    Delivered

    A market-ready product, delivered to you or straight to your customers.

You bring: idea + budgetTypical project length: 3 to 6 monthsYou receive: a product ready to sell
