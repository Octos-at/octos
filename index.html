Design

idea → plan
  • Concept & product designYour idea, turned into a clear concept, a project plan and a design that can be built.
  • Industrial designForm, ergonomics, materials and finish, shaped around the people who use the product every day.
  • 3D modelling & renderingDetailed models and photoreal renderings to settle details, pitch to investors or start marketing early.

Develop

  • FEA / FEM simulationFinite element analysis shows how parts bend and where stress builds up under load, before anything is built.
  • 3D printing, prototyping & testingPrinted parts and working prototypes, tested in-house or with accredited test facilities to the relevant standards.
  • Engineering & technical documentationEvery part engineered to high Austrian standards, then documented: drawings, CAD data, calculations, manuals.

Deliver

  • Packaging developmentPackaging that protects the product, ships well and presents your brand.

How a project runs

  1. Idea and briefing
  2. Plan and concept
  3. Industrial design and 3D models
  4. Prototypes and testing
  5. Engineering, FEA and documentation
  6. Production and supply chain
  7. Packaging
  8. Market-ready product delivered
