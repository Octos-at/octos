How it started

Founded in Innsbruck.

OCTOS was founded in 2026 by Ing. Daniel Varga, a mechanical engineer from Innsbruck. After his engineering degree at HTL Anichstraße and further studies in engineering and physics, he spent more than 12 years in the medical device industry, where he learned to design for safety and document every decision. Growing up between the mountains, with skiing, the gym and squash as constant companions, taught him focus and stamina. Today he brings it all together at OCTOS, with one ambition: never stop learning, and put every new skill to work for our clients.
