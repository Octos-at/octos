About OCTOS · The founder

Driven by curiosity. Built to last.

OCTOS was founded by Ing. Daniel Varga, a mechanical engineer from Innsbruck who turns ideas into products that work hard and feel right, for clients and under our own name.

The founder's story

Never stop learning.

Daniel was born in Innsbruck and grew up between the mountains, where sport is not a hobby but part of life. In winter you find him on skis, the rest of the year in the gym and on the squash court.

Sport taught him focus, stamina and a feel for what equipment really has to deliver.

He earned his engineering degree at HTL Anichstraße in Innsbruck, one of Tyrol's leading technical schools. Out of pure curiosity he went on to study engineering and physics at university, to understand even more deeply how materials behave and how forces flow through a structure.

For more than 12 years he has worked in the medical device industry, one of the most highly regulated fields there is. That is where he learned to design for safety, document every decision and take products through testing to recognised standards. Along the way he added furniture design, laser marking and welding technology, so ideas don't stay on the screen.

Today, at 35, he brings all of it together at OCTOS, with the same ambition he started with: never stop learning, and put every new skill to work for our clients.
