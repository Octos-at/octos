What it is

Seated, supported, and always within reach.

The OctoStool is a barber's stool on a swivel arm, mounted to the base of the barber chair. The barber stays seated and supported while moving around the client, without standing for hours or pushing a loose stool across the floor.

Health+Less strain on back, legs and shoulders
Efficiency+Stay close, precise and in control from every angle
Comfort+Full freedom and reach, fully supported
